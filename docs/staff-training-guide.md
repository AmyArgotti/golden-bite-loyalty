# Staff Training Guide

## Goal

Teach cashiers and managers how to use the Golden Bite loyalty system confidently during service.

## What Staff Need To Know

- customers log in with phone number and OTP
- each customer has a member ID, points balance, tier, and barcode
- staff can search by phone or member ID
- staff can register purchases, redeem points, and apply manual adjustments
- every point-changing action is audited

## Cashier Daily Flow

1. open the staff panel
2. search the customer by phone number or member ID
3. confirm the correct customer profile is shown
4. register the purchase amount
5. tell the customer their updated points or discount

## Redeem Flow

1. confirm the customer identity
2. check points balance
3. enter the points to redeem
4. confirm the reward was applied at checkout

## Manual Adjustment Rules

- use adjustments only when needed
- always write a clear comment
- examples:
  - service recovery
  - cashier mistake correction
  - approved manager override

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

- selecting the wrong customer before registering a purchase
- redeeming points without confirming identity
- entering the wrong purchase amount
- using manual adjustment instead of normal purchase registration

## Escalation

Escalate to the manager or admin when:

- OTP is not arriving
- points look wrong after a purchase
- a wallet pass does not update
- a customer disputes a redemption or adjustment

## Practice Checklist

- search a customer
- register a purchase
- redeem points
- add a manual adjustment with a comment
- verify the updated customer quick-view
